This app (the "App") is run by PublicCommunity, Rua das Quintinhas, 2820-623 Charneca de Caparica, Portugal ("we"). Contact for anything privacy-related: publiccommunity.app@gmail.com. We are the "controller" of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What other people can see

Your profile, posts, photos, comments, follows, the events you organise and approximate locations you add are public to other users of the App. Messages are private to the conversation. Your email, blocks, reports, feedback and notification settings are never shown to other users.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (database, accounts, file storage), EU (Ireland).
  • Expo / EAS (app packaging, over-the-air updates and push delivery). It receives your IP address when the App checks for updates, and the text of push notifications sent to you.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivers push notifications to Android phones). It receives your phone's push token and the notification text.
  • Google (sign-in with Google, if you choose it; map tiles on Android, which means Google receives your IP address and the map area you view).
  • Sentry (crash reports), EU (Germany).
  • Brevo (sends account emails), EU (France).

Some of these companies are based in the US; transfers are covered by their EU Standard Contractual Clauses / the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, profile, posts, photos, comments, messages you sent, events, likes, follows, blocks and push tokens. Reports and feedback you sent are kept without your name. Backups are kept for up to 30 days. During the test, test data may be reset.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent (e.g. turn off location or notifications in your phone's settings). Most of this is in the App (edit profile, Settings, delete account); for the rest email publiccommunity.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal: the CNPD, cnpd.pt).

Age

The App is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

We'll tell you in the App or by email before important changes to this policy.
